Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move from Vancouver to Halifax?

A cross-country move from Vancouver to Halifax typically takes 12 to 28 days depending on shipment size and scheduling. This is one of Canada's longest domestic moves at nearly 6,000 km.

How much does it cost to move from Vancouver to Halifax?

Costs for a Vancouver to Halifax move typically range from $5,000 to $15,000+ depending on home size, weight, and services required. Contact us for an accurate quote.

Do you move to Halifax from anywhere in Canada?

Yes. We move to and from Halifax from all major Canadian cities including Vancouver, Toronto, Montreal, Ottawa, Calgary, and Edmonton.

Do you use subcontractors for Halifax moves?

Never. Your belongings stay with our own trained crew from pickup to delivery — no third-party carriers, even on coast-to-coast moves.

Do you offer packing and unpacking services?

Yes, we offer full packing and unpacking services for all long-distance moves. Our team carefully packs fragile items, artwork, and electronics. Packing can be added to any quote for an additional fee.

What makes Purely Canadian Movers different?

We're family-owned since 1991 with 30+ years of experience. we do not operate like a moving broker or hand moves to unknown third-party movers — your belongings are handled by our own trained crew from pickup to delivery. We're BBB Accredited, offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees, and specialize in long-distance moves across Canada.

Do you offer storage solutions?

Yes, we offer flexible storage options for both short-term and long-term needs. Whether you need storage while waiting for your new home to be ready or temporary storage during your move, we can accommodate your requirements.
