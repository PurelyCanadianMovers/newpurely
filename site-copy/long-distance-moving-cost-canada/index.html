What is a Long-Distance Move in Canada?

A long-distance move in Canada is typically any relocation over 100 km or any move between provinces.

Examples include moving from Toronto to Vancouver, Montreal to Calgary, or Ottawa to British Columbia.

These moves require coordinated logistics, transportation scheduling, and professional handling due to the distance involved.

Quick Answer: Average Moving Costs in Canada

Long-distance moving costs in Canada typically range from $2,500 to $16,000+, depending on home size, weight, and distance.

Studio apartment moves start at $2,500.

How Long-Distance Moving Costs Are Calculated

Total weight or volume of your belongings

Distance between origin and destination

Type of home (studio, apartment, house)

Access conditions (stairs, elevators, rural access)

Packing, storage, or specialty services

Seasonal demand (peak summer vs off-season)

Why Long-Distance Moves Cost More

Long-distance moves require fuel, labour, coordination, and time across thousands of kilometres. Unlike local moves, shipments must be scheduled, tracked, and sometimes consolidated with other loads to ensure efficiency. Proper insurance, equipment, and planning are essential for safe delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to move from Toronto to Vancouver?

Most moves range from $2,200 to $12,000+ depending on home size, weight, and services required.

How long does a cross-country move take in Canada?

Transit times vary by route: Vancouver–Toronto is 9–22 days, Toronto–Calgary is 7–19 days, and Montreal–Vancouver is 10–22 days. Exact timing depends on shipment size and scheduling.

What is included in a long-distance move?

Transportation, loading, unloading, and optional packing or storage services.

Are long-distance moves insured?

Yes, professional movers provide insurance coverage options for protection during transit.
