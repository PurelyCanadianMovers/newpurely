How is the cost of a long-distance move calculated?

Costs are based on weight, distance, and additional services such as packing or storage.

Are my items insured during the move?

Yes, we offer coverage options to protect your belongings throughout the move.

Can you move from Vancouver to smaller towns across Canada?

Yes, we service both major cities and rural locations across Canada.

Do you offer packing and unpacking services for Vancouver long-distance moves?

Yes, we offer full packing and unpacking services for all long-distance moves. Our team carefully packs fragile items, artwork, and electronics. Packing can be added to any quote for an additional fee.
