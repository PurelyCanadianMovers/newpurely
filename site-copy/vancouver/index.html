Vancouver moving cost and planning factors

Vancouver moving prices depend on the size of the move, crew size, truck time, access, stairs, elevators, walking distance, parking, packing, storage, valuation coverage, and your move date. Condo and apartment moves can also be affected by elevator reservations, loading dock rules, strata move windows, and downtown access.

Planning tip: If your building has a service elevator, loading zone, or limited parking, confirm those details before requesting an estimate. Better access details help the team price the correct crew, truck, and timing.
