What Makes an Edmonton–Toronto Move Unique

Distance and logistics: At roughly 2800 km, this is a true cross-country move. Scheduling, weight estimates, and delivery windows all require more precision than a shorter haul. Our team walks you through every detail before your move date.

Toronto's climate: Toronto's humid summers and cold winters require careful climate control. We protect sensitive items and plan around seasonal weather patterns.

Elevator bookings and condo rules: Both Edmonton and Toronto have large condo populations with strict elevator booking windows and loading dock rules. We coordinate with building management in advance to avoid delays on move day.

Employment-driven relocations: Many Edmonton-to-Toronto moves are corporate or employment-driven. We work with individuals and HR departments to accommodate tight timelines and provide the documentation needed for relocation reimbursement.

Tips for Moving to Toronto

Book Your Condo Elevator Early

Toronto has thousands of high-rise condos with strict elevator booking windows. Contact your building manager at least 2 weeks before your move date. We can help coordinate the booking.

Parking Permits for Moving Trucks

Toronto requires a parking permit for moving trucks on city streets. Apply through the City of Toronto's online portal at least 5 business days before your move. We can advise on the process.

Update Your Ontario Health Card

You have 3 months after establishing Ontario residency to apply for OHIP. Visit a ServiceOntario location with proof of identity and residency. Cancel your Alberta Health Card after your new coverage begins.

Toronto Neighbourhoods

Toronto's downtown core (King West, Distillery District, Leslieville) has narrow streets and limited parking. Suburban areas (Scarborough, Etobicoke, North York) offer easier truck access. Let us know your destination so we can plan accordingly.

Ontario Land Transfer Tax

If you're buying a home in Toronto, be aware of both the Ontario and Toronto municipal land transfer taxes. First-time buyers may qualify for rebates. Factor these into your budget when planning your move.

What is the best time of year to move from Edmonton to Toronto?

Late spring (May–June) and early fall (September–October) are ideal. Summer is peak season — book 6–8 weeks in advance. Winter moves are possible but require extra planning for Edmonton's extreme cold at the origin end.

What should I know about Toronto before moving there?

Toronto is Canada's largest city and a major financial and cultural hub. It has a higher cost of living than Edmonton, but also more diverse job opportunities, world-class transit, and a vibrant multicultural community. Be prepared for higher housing costs and traffic.

What should I do before the movers arrive?

Defrost your fridge 24 hours ahead, disconnect appliances, label boxes by room, and set aside essentials (medications, chargers, documents) in a bag you'll carry yourself. If you're in a condo, book the elevator and loading dock in advance.
