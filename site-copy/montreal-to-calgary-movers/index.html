Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move from Montreal to Calgary?

A Montreal to Calgary move typically takes 7–19 days. The delivery window depends on shipment size, scheduling, consolidation, weather, and access at pickup and delivery.

How much does it cost to move from Montreal to Calgary?

Planning estimates are Studio $2,500+, 1-bedroom $4,700+, 2-bedroom $6,300+, 3-bedroom $10,000+, and 4+ bedroom $15,000+. Final pricing depends on weight or volume, access, packing, storage, season, and services.

How far is Montreal from Calgary by road?

The Montreal–Calgary moving corridor is approximately 3,600 km by road. Exact routing can vary with pickup and delivery access, weather, and operating conditions.

Do you also move from Calgary to Montreal?

Yes. Purely Canadian Movers coordinates this interprovincial long-distance route in both directions.

Can you help with packing for a Montreal to Calgary move?

Yes. Full and partial packing, specialty packing, unpacking, and packing-material removal can be included in a written estimate.

What should I plan for at my Montreal pickup?

Confirm parking, loading access, stairs, and elevator reservations early. Dense streets and apartment access can affect crew timing and the written estimate.

What should I plan for at my Calgary delivery?

Confirm loading access and elevator bookings, and plan for rapid weather changes. Share access restrictions before the move so delivery can be scheduled realistically.

What protection options are available?

Ask the moving consultant about Declared Value Protection options and review the selected protection, inventory, access, and services in the written estimate before booking.
