Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move from Montreal to Victoria or Nanaimo?

A Montreal to Victoria or Nanaimo move typically takes 10–22 days for delivery, depending on the size of your shipment, scheduling, and the ferry crossing to Vancouver Island. We will give you a realistic delivery window when you book your move.

How much does it cost to move from Montreal to Victoria or Nanaimo?

The cost depends on the weight of your shipment and the distance. A studio or 1-bedroom typically starts around $3,000 to $5,300+, while a 3–4 bedroom home can range from $11,000 to $16,000+. The island surcharge reflects the BC Ferries crossing. Contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.

Do you move from Victoria or Nanaimo to Montreal as well?

Yes — we handle moves in both directions. Whether you are moving from Montreal to Vancouver Island or from Victoria/Nanaimo to Montreal, our team provides the same professional, fully insured service.

Why is moving to Vancouver Island more expensive than moving to Vancouver?

Moves to Vancouver Island require a BC Ferries crossing from the Lower Mainland, which adds time, logistics complexity, and ferry costs to the shipment. This is reflected in the slightly higher pricing compared to mainland BC destinations.

Are you a direct carrier or do you use subcontractors?

We do not operate like a moving broker or hand moves to unknown third-party movers. As agents of Great Canadian Van Lines, your move is coordinated through Purely Canadian Movers and the Great Canadian Van Lines agent network, with clear documentation, Declared Value Protection options, and no broker-style handoffs to unknown movers.

Do you provide packing services for a Montreal–Victoria/Nanaimo move?

Yes. We offer full packing and unpacking services, including specialty packing for fragile items, artwork, and electronics. Packing can be added to any long-distance move quote.

Is my shipment insured during a Montreal to Victoria/Nanaimo move?

All shipments are covered by basic Declared Value Protection. We strongly recommend discussing additional Declared Value Protection options with our team before your move date.

What is the best time of year to move from Montreal to Vancouver Island?

Late spring (May–June) and early fall (September–October) are ideal. Summer is peak season on Vancouver Island — book 4–6 weeks in advance. The island's mild climate means year-round moving is feasible, but summer ferry traffic can cause scheduling delays.

Can I get a written estimate for my Montreal to Victoria/Nanaimo move?

Yes. We provide written estimates based on an in-home or virtual inventory assessment. A written estimate explains the expected price range and services included before moving day.

Do you offer storage if my Victoria or Nanaimo home isn't ready?

Yes. We offer both short-term and long-term storage options. If your new home on Vancouver Island isn't ready on arrival day, we can hold your belongings securely on the mainland and deliver when you're ready.

Do I need to update my health card when moving from Quebec to BC?

Yes. You'll need to cancel your Quebec RAMQ health card and apply for BC's Medical Services Plan (MSP) within 3 months of establishing residency. There is a 3-month waiting period for MSP coverage, so consider private health insurance in the interim.

What makes Purely Canadian Movers different from other long-distance movers?

We're family-owned since 1991 with 30+ years of experience on cross-country Canadian routes. We do not operate like a moving broker or hand moves to unknown third-party movers, are BBB Accredited, offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees, and provide a dedicated point of contact throughout your move.
