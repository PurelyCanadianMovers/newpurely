Moving Tips for Your Vancouver Island Arrival

Plan for the BC Ferries Crossing

All shipments to Vancouver Island travel via BC Ferries from the Lower Mainland. We coordinate the ferry crossing as part of your move — no action needed on your part, but it does add 10–22 days to the overall timeline.

Victoria's Mild Climate Is Year-Round Moving Friendly

Victoria has the mildest climate in Canada. Unlike Montreal, you won't face extreme cold or heavy snowfall. However, summer ferry traffic is heavy — book early if moving June through August.

Update Your BC Health Card

Apply for BC's Medical Services Plan (MSP) immediately upon arrival. There is a 3-month waiting period, so arrange private health insurance to cover the gap after cancelling your Quebec RAMQ coverage.

Parking and Access in Victoria

Victoria's older neighbourhoods often have narrow streets and limited parking for large moving trucks. Let us know your address in advance so we can plan the best access route and equipment.

Nanaimo as an Alternative to Victoria

Nanaimo is a growing city on Vancouver Island with lower housing costs than Victoria. If you're still deciding between the two, note that both are served by BC Ferries from Tsawwassen or Horseshoe Bay.
