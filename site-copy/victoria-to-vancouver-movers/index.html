Moving from Victoria, BC to Vancouver, BC

Moving from Victoria on Vancouver Island to Vancouver in the Lower Mainland is an intra-BC intercity move that depends on coordinated pickup, ferry transportation, and destination access. A written estimate aligns the shipment, services, access details, and timing before moving day.

Victoria pickup planning may include apartment or condo elevators, strata move windows, loading areas, parking, stairs, long carries, and the time needed to reach the ferry terminal. Share the complete origin details when requesting an estimate.

Vancouver delivery planning may include elevator reservations, loading-zone restrictions, parking, building move hours, and strata requirements. Ferry availability, shipment size, weather, and access can affect the confirmed schedule, so the 1–3 day range is planning guidance rather than a guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Victoria to Vancouver move take?

Typical transit planning is 1–3 days. Ferry availability, terminal conditions, shipment size, pickup and delivery access, weather, and requested services can affect the confirmed schedule.

How much does it cost to move from Victoria to Vancouver?

Current planning estimates are $1,200+ for a studio, $1,600+ for a 1-bedroom, $2,200+ for a 2-bedroom, $3,000+ for a 3-bedroom, and $4,200+ for a 4+ bedroom home. These are CAD planning estimates, not guaranteed prices.

How are ferry logistics handled?

The moving plan accounts for Vancouver Island departure, ferry transportation, and Lower Mainland arrival. Sailing availability and terminal conditions vary, so exact ferry or delivery timing is confirmed for the move rather than guaranteed on this page.

What access details should I provide?

Provide Victoria pickup and Vancouver delivery addresses, strata or building rules, elevator reservations, loading and parking restrictions, stairs, long carries, and any required move windows.

Do you provide packing and unpacking services?

Yes. Full or partial packing and unpacking can be included in the written estimate, including appropriate planning for fragile items, artwork, and electronics.

Can storage be included?

Yes. Short-term or longer-term storage can be planned when the origin and destination dates do not align. Requested storage is identified in the written estimate.

What protection applies to the shipment?

Basic carrier liability applies. Available Declared Value Protection options can be reviewed and documented with the written estimate before moving day.

Who coordinates the move?

Purely Canadian Movers coordinates the move with Great Canadian Van Lines agent-network support where applicable. Responsibilities, services, and estimate details are documented in writing rather than assigned through an unknown moving broker.
