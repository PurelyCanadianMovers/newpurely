Moving Estimate FAQs

How do I get a free moving estimate?

Fill out the contact form or call 1-877-485-6683. Purely Canadian Movers reviews your move details and follows up with a no-obligation estimate.

What information should I include in my moving quote request?

Include your moving date, origin and destination, home or office size, access details, packing needs, storage needs, and any heavy, fragile, or specialty items.

Do you provide local and long-distance moving estimates?

Yes. We provide estimates for local moves in Metro Vancouver, long-distance moves across Canada, cross-country relocations, office moves, packing, storage, and Canada-USA moving.

Which areas do you serve from this contact page?

We serve Vancouver, Coquitlam, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond, North Vancouver, Langley, New Westminster, Delta, Port Moody, White Rock, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows, and long-distance routes across Canada.

How quickly will someone contact me?

Most estimate requests receive a response within one business day. For urgent moves, call 1-877-485-6683 or 604-522-7222.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. Purely Canadian Movers is family-owned, based in Coquitlam, and uses its own trained moving crews rather than subcontractors.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do Purely Canadian Movers serve?

We serve Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland for local moves — including Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Burnaby, Vancouver, Surrey, Langley, Maple Ridge, New Westminster, North Vancouver, Richmond, Delta, White Rock, and Abbotsford — plus long-distance routes across Canada and to the USA.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. Local moves are handled by our own trained crews, and long-distance moves run through the Great Canadian Van Lines network rather than a broker handoff to unknown movers.
