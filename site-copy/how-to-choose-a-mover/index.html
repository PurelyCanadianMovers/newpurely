How to Choose a Reputable Mover in Canada

Choosing a reputable moving company is the most important step in the moving process. Learn what the Government of Canada and the Canadian Association of Movers recommend — and how to identify red flags.

Government of Canada Standards

The Government of Canada's Office of Consumer Affairs provides clear guidance on choosing a mover. Here are the key standards every reputable moving company should meet:

Get Multiple Estimates

Get estimates from at least three reputable movers and be clear about what you want them to include. Written estimates are essential.

Check CAM Certification

Verify the mover is certified by the Canadian Association of Movers (CAM). CAM members meet strict industry standards and professional practices.

Verify Local Presence

Ensure they have a physical address and can be found in the event of a problem. Avoid companies without a local presence.

Check References

Ask for and contact references from previous customers. A reputable mover will have no problem providing them.

Confirm Insurance Coverage

Ask about liability coverage and valuation options for high-value items. Ensure your belongings will be protected during the move.

Understand Their Operations

Find out how the company manages your move and protects your belongings. Ask about their process and what to expect.

Source: Government of Canada, Office of Consumer Affairs, Moving Advice (ised-isde.canada.ca)

What CAM Certification Means

The Canadian Association of Movers (CAM) is Canada's only trade association representing moving and storage companies. CAM members are vetted and must meet strict membership requirements.

Industry Leadership

CAM members are Canada's top moving companies with proven track records.

Professional Standards

Members commit to ethical practices, fair treatment, and delivering promised services.

Accountability

Membership requires Board approval and ongoing compliance with CAM standards.

Purely Canadian Movers is a proud CAM member. We've been vetted by the Canadian Association of Movers and meet all membership standards for professional conduct and customer service.

Red Flags to Avoid

The Government of Canada warns consumers to watch for these warning signs when choosing a mover:

No physical address or can't be found online

Estimates given only over the phone, not in writing

Requesting a large deposit upfront

Not listed by CAM or Better Business Bureau

Using subcontractors (major source of consumer complaints)

Unwilling to provide references

Unclear pricing or hidden fees

About Subcontractors: The Government of Canada specifically warns that sub-contracting is a major source of consumer complaints. It can result in a confusing trail of responsibility if a dispute arises, and many brokers do not assume responsibility for lost or broken items. Frequently Asked Questions

What does CAM membership mean?

The Canadian Association of Movers (CAM) is Canada's only trade association representing moving and storage companies. CAM members are vetted by the organization's Board of Directors and must meet strict membership requirements. Membership indicates the company treats customers fairly, delivers promised services, and maintains professional standards.

Why is no-subcontractor important?

According to the Government of Canada, sub-contracting is a major source of consumer complaints. When a mover uses subcontractors, there can be a confusing trail of responsibility if something goes wrong. With Purely Canadian Movers, every mover on your job is a direct employee — you know exactly who's handling your belongings.

What should I ask about insurance?

Ask about the mover's liability coverage and what it covers. Ask about valuation options for high-value items like artwork, antiques, or electronics. Make sure you understand what's covered and what's not before signing any agreement.

Is BBB accreditation important?

Yes. BBB accreditation indicates the company meets standards for ethical business practices, customer satisfaction, and transparent advertising. A BBB A+ rating demonstrates a strong commitment to customer service and ethical conduct.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for local moves, and 4–8 weeks for long-distance or cross-country moves. However, we do our best to accommodate last-minute bookings when availability allows.
